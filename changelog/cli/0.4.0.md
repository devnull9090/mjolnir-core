# Classic map lightmaps re-solved on your GPU, at any resolution

**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Summary:** Re-solve a classic Halo CE map's lightmaps with its original radiosity, at several times the shipped resolution and on your GPU, plus per-game-type vehicles.

This release brings the lighting behind the re-lit classic maps: the CLI now
solves a classic map's lightmaps itself, the way the original level tools
did, at a resolution of your choosing. It also carries the converted maps'
per-game-type vehicles and scenery that stops bullets.

### Added

- **`mjolnir level lightmaps <staging> <scene.gltf> <out>`** solves a
  classic map's lightmaps with Halo CE's own radiosity: the sky's sun and
  fill, the light that glowing surfaces give off, and the light bouncing
  between surfaces. It writes pages at a multiple of the shipped pages' size
  (`--scale auto` picks the largest that stays within budget, up to 2048 a
  side), drawing the sun and fill at every texel so shadow edges stay crisp.
  Two companion pages per lightmap page record where the sun reaches and the
  light without the sun, for the converted maps' Unreal lighting.
- **It runs on your GPU.** The rays go through the graphics card's ray
  tracing hardware where it has some (Vulkan), otherwise through a compute
  shader (Vulkan, DirectX 12 or Metal), and the solve falls back to the CPU
  when there is no usable graphics card. On an RTX 5090 Blood Gulch solves
  in 9 seconds and Gephyrophobia in about 2 minutes (under 5 on 32 CPU
  threads). `--cpu` keeps everything on the processor.
- **Options for the curious:** `--compare` scores a solve against the
  map's shipped lightmaps, `--batch` trades a little fidelity for speed,
  `--texel-elements` stops patches from getting finer than the page can
  show, `--element-edges` draws the patches over each page, and `--verbose`
  reports where the time and the light went.
- **`mjolnir megalo write --vehicle-label-pool`** and **`--require-label`**
  write game types that place each team's vehicles the way Halo CE's game
  variants did, so a converted map no longer spawns every game type's
  vehicles at once.

### Changed

- **Scenery blocks bullets.** `mjolnir level collision` now builds a placed
  object's collision into the level's collision tree, so projectiles hit
  Covenant shields and similar scenery that players and vehicles already
  collided with.
