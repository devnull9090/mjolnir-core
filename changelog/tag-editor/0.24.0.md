# Clearer help text, and block indexes named for what they point at

**Date:** 2026-10-24
**Summary:** The tag editor names what a block index points at and what a squad cell spawns, and its help and messages now match what it does.

### Changed

- **Block indexes say what they point at,** and a squad's cells say what
  they spawn, instead of showing bare numbers.
- **Help and messages** were rewritten to match what the editor does today.
