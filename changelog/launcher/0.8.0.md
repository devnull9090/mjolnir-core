# See who made it

**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Summary:** Click an author's name anywhere and their profile opens in the launcher: their avatar, everything they have published, and what that work has drawn.

Every mod said who made it, and that was as far as it went. If a texture pack
was good there was no way to ask "what else have they done?" without going to
the website and searching by hand.

### Added

- **Profiles.** Click an author's name anywhere — a mod byline, a comment, a
  review — and their page opens in the launcher, without sending you to a
  browser. Their Discord avatar, when they joined, and every mod they have
  published, each one installable from where it sits.
- Two sets of figures, kept apart because they say different things: what their
  published work drew — mods published, downloads, page views, and an average
  rating weighted by how many people voted, so one five-star rating cannot
  outweigh a hundred four-star ones — and what they have done around the
  hub: mods downloaded, ratings given, comments, screenshots contributed.
- Discord avatars beside every author name, on mod cards and mod pages alike.
- **Open on mjolnircore.com** from a profile, the same escape hatch mod pages
  already had.

### Changed

- Installing a mod now tells the hub it was you, so your own "mods downloaded"
  count reflects what you install from here. It counts distinct mods, not
  installs — reinstalling something is not a new mod. Nothing records *which*
  mods anyone downloaded, and no page shows anyone's library; the number is a
  number.

### Notes

- The count starts at zero for everyone. Downloads before this release were
  never attributed to an account, and nothing invents a history that was not
  recorded.
