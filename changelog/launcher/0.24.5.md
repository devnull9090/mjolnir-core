# Refresh your name from Discord

**Date:** 2026-11-26
**Summary:** Changed your Discord name or avatar? The account menu's new Refresh from Discord brings the launcher, the hub and the game up to date.

### Added

- **Refresh from Discord.** The account menu (your name at the top right)
  has a new item. It opens a Discord sign-in in your browser, and when you
  come back to the launcher your name and avatar are the current ones. Your
  name in multiplayer is your hub name, so this is also how you change it in
  game.

### Fixed

- **An old name in the account menu.** The launcher kept the name and avatar
  from when you first signed in. It now checks them with the hub each time it
  checks your sign-in.
