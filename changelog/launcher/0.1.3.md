# Fixes a startup crash, and brands the installer

**Date:** 2026-07-25
**Summary:** Fixes a crash that stopped the launcher opening at all on some installs, and gives the Windows installer proper MJOLNIR branding.

### Fixed

- The launcher could crash on startup instead of opening its window. The update
  system was configured without the public key it verifies downloads against,
  and failing to find one brought the whole app down.

### Changed

- The Windows installer carries MJOLNIR header and sidebar artwork, and its
  final screen links straight to the Discord community.
