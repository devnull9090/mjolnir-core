# Maps hand out their own starting weapons

**Date:** 2026-11-28
**Summary:** A converted map can now give players the starting weapons and grenades its original map did, per game type, instead of an assault rifle everywhere.

### Added

In MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.22.0.

- **Starting weapons from the map.** Halo 1 and Halo 2 maps say what a
  player spawns holding, and Halo 1's say it per game type: an SMG on most
  Halo 2 maps, a plasma rifle on Midship and Waterworks, a battle rifle and
  SMG on Tombstone; on Blood Gulch an assault rifle and pistol in CTF and a
  plasma pistol in everything else. A map whose pack carries that now spawns
  you with it, with the original's grenades. A host's own game settings
  still apply on top.
- Nothing changes on a map whose pack does not carry a starting kit: you
  spawn with the assault rifle, as before. The map packs that carry kits are
  released separately.
