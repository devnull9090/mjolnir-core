# Weapons lit without ray tracing

**Date:** 2026-11-27
**Summary:** On graphics cards without hardware ray tracing, such as a GTX 1080 Ti, your first-person weapon drew black. It is lit again.

### Fixed

In MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.21.12.

- **Black first-person weapon on cards without hardware ray tracing.** At
  the Low and Medium video settings the game stops updating the sky light,
  and it kept a capture taken before the map had drawn: black. Without ray
  tracing the sky light is all that lights your weapon, so it drew black.
  The loader now captures the sky again once the map is ready. On a
  GTX 1080 Ti at Battle Creek, the weapon went from black to properly lit.
  Cards with ray tracing look as before.
