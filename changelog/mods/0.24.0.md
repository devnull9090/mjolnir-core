# The remastered classic maps

**Date:** 2026-10-17
**Summary:** The level loader draws the remastered classic maps: real terrain and grass, the game's own rock and trees, a living ocean and rivers, and Halo CE's lamps lit by Unreal.

### Added

- **Remastered maps** (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.7.0). Ten classic maps now
  draw the way Campaign Evolved draws its own levels. The ground is an
  Unreal Engine landscape with the campaign's sand, earth and grass, and
  grass and pebbles grow across it in the wind. Cliffs are drawn in the
  game's own rock, with its stones set into the walls, and Halo CE's
  boulders and trees are swapped for the campaign's own.
- **Water that moves.** Death Island and Yoyorast Island sit in a real
  ocean whose swell breaks on the beach. Timberland and Battle Creek get
  rivers that flow along their beds and over their falls.
- **Tyre tracks and footprints** in the sand and snow of the remastered
  maps, as on the campaign's beaches.
- **Snow.** Falling snow on Ice Fields and Sidewinder, kept out of tunnels
  and rooms.
- **Halo CE's lamps, lit by Unreal.** The light fixtures CE placed on the
  remastered maps cast real light and shadow.

### Fixed

- **Sunlight through inside corners.** A thin sliver of sun no longer
  crawls along the corners where walls meet on converted maps.
- **Shadows on the ground** no longer change shape as you walk across the
  new terrain.
