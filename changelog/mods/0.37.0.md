# Reload and grenade sounds for players who join a match under way

**Date:** 2026-10-24
**Summary:** A player who joined a match in progress heard gunfire but no reload, grenade or other sound attached to an object; the lobby now gives their game the audio binding the join skipped.

### Fixed

- **Silent reloads and grenades for joiners** (MJOLNIRLobby 0.17.1). The
  game binds its audio layer to a match's audio world from a world listener
  that runs when a match starts normally. A player who joins a match already
  under way builds their game another way and that listener never runs, so
  the engine dropped every object-attached sound before it reached Wwise:
  reloads, grenade pins and throws, melee, equipment. Gunfire still played
  because it takes another path. The lobby now performs that bind itself once
  the joiner's game is running; the host and players who were in the fireteam
  at the start were never affected and are left alone.

### Added

- A sound tracer for the next audio bug: with `MJOLNIRLobby/native/sound_trace.txt`
  present when the game starts, every sound the simulation asks for, every
  Wwise event posted and the audio binds are written to `sound_trace.log`
  (`docs/re/client_sound_roles.md`).
