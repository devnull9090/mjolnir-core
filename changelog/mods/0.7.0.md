# Classic Halo CE multiplayer, in alpha

**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Summary:** A MULTIPLAYER menu, the classic CE maps with Slayer and CTF for your fireteam, and a multiplayer HUD with a kill feed and a Tab scoreboard. Alpha.

The nineteen original Halo: Combat Evolved multiplayer maps, rebuilt for Halo
Campaign Evolved, can now be played with your fireteam. The maps themselves
come from the hub: install them from the launcher's **Multiplayer** page. This
set holds the code that starts them and the screens you play them from.

This is an alpha. Expect rough edges, and tell us what breaks.

### Added

- **MJOLNIRLobby 0.1.0** adds **MULTIPLAYER** to the main menu. Pick a map and
  a game type, invite friends from your fireteam, and start. The lobby and map
  select are screens of our own, cooked into the game rather than painted over
  its menus.
- **MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.1.0** starts a converted map under the game's
  multiplayer rules engine, dresses it with its terrain, sky and lights, and
  brings friends who join into the same map and game type. It also places the
  CTF flags and health packs and plays the announcer.
- **MJOLNIRHud 0.1.0** draws the multiplayer HUD: a kill feed with the weapon
  used, the respawn countdown, a scoreboard on Tab (the back button on a
  controller), and name tags over teammates in team games only.

### Changed

- **MJOLNIRConsoleEnabler 1.1.0** opens the console on the tilde key. Tab is
  the scoreboard now.
- MJOLNIRBridge 0.1.1, MJOLNIRMapMenu 0.1.1, MJOLNIRMultiplayer 0.2.1 and
  MJOLNIRWorldBuilder 0.1.1 carry small fixes from the same work.

### Known issues

- Fireteams are limited to four players for now. Raising the cap is next.
- At the end of a game everyone returns to the main menu. A post-game screen
  with a vote for the next map is planned.
- Finding public games from inside the game is not there yet. Invite friends
  through your fireteam, or see live games on mjolnircore.com/games.
