# Benches that fill the screen

**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Summary:** `/bench` and `mjolnir_bench` now measure the whole screen at your own field of view, so their timings are right on monitors that aren't 16:9.

### Fixed

- **Benches see the whole screen** (MJOLNIRHud 0.11.3). On a screen that
  isn't 16:9, `/bench` and `mjolnir_bench` drew the held view with black
  bars, so their timings read about 10% light on a 16:10 monitor. The held
  view now fills the screen at your own field of view.
