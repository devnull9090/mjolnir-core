# A marker on the ball, and teams that agree on every PC

**Date:** 2026-11-07
**Summary:** Oddball gets a marker on the ball, markers follow players smoothly and into vehicles, and joiners' scoreboards take each team from the host.

### Added

In MJOLNIRHud 0.18.0.

- **A marker on the ball in Oddball.** Nothing used to say where the ball was.
  It now has a skull marker with its distance: gold while it lies loose, orange
  while someone carries it, and none on the carrier's own screen.

### Changed

- **The Juggernaut's beacon floats over their head.** In Juggernaut and Reverse
  Tag the beacon used to rise from the holder's feet. It now starts above their
  head, and the holder no longer sees their own. Their screen already says YOU
  ARE THE JUGGERNAUT, or YOU ARE IT.
- **MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.12.3** stages a test copy's game type in that copy's
  own saved folder. Nothing changes for players.

### Fixed

In MJOLNIRHud 0.18.0.

- **Beacons and markers lagged behind.** The Juggernaut's beacon and marker,
  and the flag markers, moved ten times a second and jumped along behind
  whoever they followed. They now move every frame.
- **A marker stayed where a player got into a vehicle.** A Juggernaut or Reverse
  Tag marker, and a hill's contested state, kept the spot where the player
  boarded. They now follow the vehicle.
- **Players under AWAITING ASSIGNMENT, or on the wrong team.** On a joiner's
  scoreboard, a player who joined late, or anyone past the first two seats,
  could show under AWAITING ASSIGNMENT. A player could also be listed on the
  other team. The host's game knows each team, and every joiner now takes the
  teams from it.
- **Bots from an earlier match.** Bots added in one match came back on a
  joiner's scoreboard in later matches, under AWAITING ASSIGNMENT. A match now
  lists only its own bots.
