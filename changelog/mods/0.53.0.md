# Your own sound mix, and maps that sound like Halo 2

**Date:** 2026-11-18
**Summary:** Type /sounds to set each of a map's sounds to your liking. Halo 2 maps get their waves, birds and wind, and remastered maps load faster.

### Added

In MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.20.0 and MJOLNIRHud 0.22.0.

- **A sound mixer.** Type `/sounds` in chat to open a panel with a slider
  for every sound on the map, plus one for all of them. A dot shows which
  ones you can hear from where you stand. Your levels are kept for each map
  on your PC.
- **Halo 2's ambient details.** The one-off sounds Halo 2 plays at random
  now come through: waves on Zanzibar's shore, gulls, birds, a distant
  battle, gusts of wind. Ambient sound on Halo 2 maps is louder too, so you
  can hear it next to the game's own mix.
- **Halo 2 machines in their own materials.** Zanzibar's gate and wheels
  draw with materials built into the map, the same for every player.

### Changed

- **Faster remastered maps.** Rocks on the cliffs wear materials built
  into the map instead of being set one by one as it loads, which took
  over a second on Ascension.

### Fixed

- **Zanzibar's big gear stays drawn.** It disappeared from some angles, as
  did door parts on Containment's gate.
- **No crash after switching maps.** Going from one remastered map to
  another could crash host and client together.
- **Death Island's horizon.** The dark line where the sea meets the sky is
  gone, and the bottom of the sky no longer flashes.
- **Ascension's floating rocks.** The fog under them is back, so you no
  longer see their undersides.
- **Containment's canyon walls** are lit by bounced light instead of
  fading to black.
- **Moving parts on a second match.** Doors and gears drew in a grey grid
  material when a map was played twice in a row.
