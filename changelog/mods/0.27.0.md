# Match chat

**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Summary:** Multiplayer matches have text chat, with team chat, slash commands and hub avatars. Every line is kept for moderators, and public matches show their chat on the hub.

### Added

- **Match chat** (MJOLNIRHud 0.9.0). Press **T** for all chat, **Y** for
  team chat, or **Home**. **Enter** sends and **Esc** cancels. Lines appear
  above the kill feed with the sender's hub avatar and team colour, then fade
  after a few seconds. While you type, your player stands still.
- **Chat rules.** The first time you open chat, it shows the rules and asks
  you to agree. Chat is recorded and logged to your player account, and
  public matches post their chat to the hub. Keep it civil.
- **Slash commands.** Type `/` in chat to see the commands you can use, and
  **Tab** to complete one or a player's name.
  - Everyone: `/help`, `/players`, and `/suicide` to respawn when you're
    stuck.
  - The host: `/kick`, `/ban`, `/unban` and `/slay`, plus `/tp` and `/bring`
    to teleport (experimental).
- **Chat for moderators.** The host keeps every line of a match, private
  matches included, and sends it to the hub (MJOLNIRLobby 0.13.3). A player
  report shows its match's chat to moderators. A public match's page on the
  hub shows its chat too.

### Fixed

- **A host crash after a player left** (MJOLNIRHud 0.8.1, in this release).
  The HUD read the team of a player's body after the game had freed it, which
  crashed the host on Blood Gulch about 45 seconds after a client left.
- **Kicking by name** (MJOLNIRLobby 0.13.3). Kicking "Bob" no longer kicks
  "Bobby" when Bobby is listed first.

### Notes

- Chat needs **CE runtime 1.11.0**, which the launcher installs with the
  maps. Without it the HUD runs as before, with no chat.
- The game's own chat key (Home) used Xbox party chat, which never connects
  in MJOLNIR games. That is why it never showed anything.
