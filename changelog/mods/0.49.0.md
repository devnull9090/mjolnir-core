# Spawns by game type, ready for the maps

**Date:** 2026-11-08
**Summary:** The loader can give each game type its own spawn points, CTF flags cost far less to wave, and hosts stutter far less, with or without bots.

### Changed

In MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.13.2.

- **Spawns by game type, ready for the maps.** A converted map can now give
  each game type its own spawn points, as CE and Halo 2 did: Slayer at
  Slayer's starts and not in the CTF bases, CTF only in the bases, and Race,
  Oddball and King at their own starts. Maps change once they are updated;
  until then they play as before.
- **Lighter flags in Capture the Flag and Assault.** A flag's cloth was moved
  30 times a second whenever you were within 50 m of it, on screen or not,
  and the loop ran on every map. A flag now waves only while you can see it:
  15 times a second at rest, still 30 while it's carried so its trail swings
  round smoothly, and maps without flags are checked once a second. On Chill
  Out, with no flag carried, the cloth now takes 1–6 ms of each second
  instead of 11–16. It has six panels instead of eight.
- **Flags waved by your graphics card.** With a CE runtime pack that carries
  the new flag cloth material, the cloth waves on the graphics card: the
  loader moves nothing while a flag stands still and only tells the material
  which way a carried flag trails. On Chill Out that is under 1 ms of each
  second at rest and about 3 while you carry a flag, down from about 5 and 11.
  Until that runtime pack is out, flags wave as in the entry above.

### Fixed

In MJOLNIRLobby 0.24.1.

- **A stutter every five seconds while hosting.** Every five seconds the host
  searched the whole game for the players' connections, even with nobody
  else in the game, and each search made a frame of 50–70 ms. The host now
  searches again only when someone joins or leaves, or the map changes. It
  also stops re-reading the launcher's list of installed maps when that list
  hasn't changed.

In MJOLNIRHud 0.19.1 and MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.13.2.

- **Stutters when hosting with bots.** When bots started, and again whenever
  one was slow to appear, the host searched the whole game for every kind of
  bot body. Each search froze the game for up to a third of a second. The HUD
  now notes each body as the game makes it and never searches. On Blood Gulch
  with eight bots, the HUD's longest moment went from 308 ms to 10 ms.
  In King with eight bots, it went from 58 ms to 8 ms.
- **Bots cost the host less all the time.** Four times a second the host
  checked where every body the game keeps ready for bots was standing, 271 of
  them on Blood Gulch. It now checks only the bodies in play, and the rest
  only while a bot is coming in. With eight bots the HUD's share of the game
  went from 11.6 to 5.0 ms each second.
- **Less work in King, CTF, Assault and Oddball.** Drawing King's hill looked
  up the same engine objects for every panel of its wall, and the flag and
  ball markers did the same for every flag, five times a second. Each lookup
  is now made once.
- **A stutter every two seconds on maps with gates and shields.** When one of
  a map's gates or shields never found its machine, the loader searched the
  whole game for it every two seconds, for the rest of the match, at 25 ms a
  search (seen on Yoyorast). It now waits longer after each search that finds
  nothing, and stops after eight.
