# Join a match already under way

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Summary:** Join a public game while its match is under way, straight from FIND GAMES. Hosts get a HOST MENU to end the match, return to the lobby, and kick or ban players.

### Added

- **Join in progress.** A public game takes new players while its match is
  running. Pick it in FIND GAMES and you load into the match, with the
  host's map and game type, the HUD and the scoreboard; the host's game puts
  your Spartan in and spawns you within seconds. A private game still keeps
  latecomers out.
- **HOST MENU.** The host's pause menu has a HOST MENU entry during a match:
  END GAME (results and the vote on the next game, as after any match),
  RETURN TO LOBBY (everyone goes back to the lobby to change the map or game
  type), PLAYERS (kick or ban) and BANNED PLAYERS (unban). A banned player
  who joins again is sent back to their menu. The campaign-only RELOAD
  CHECKPOINT and RESTART MISSION are gone from the pause menu in a
  multiplayer match.

### Fixed

- **A public host alone keeps playing.** When the other players leave, the
  match goes on instead of ending, and others can still join it.
- **A host alone stays joinable.** A public match started by its host alone
  keeps its online lobby, so FIND GAMES can join it.
- **Players who join hear their own events.** Teleporters, the respawn
  countdown and health packs now play for every player, not only the host.

### Known issues

- Rejoining the same match right after leaving or being kicked can stall on
  the loading screen. Back out and join again after a minute or two.
