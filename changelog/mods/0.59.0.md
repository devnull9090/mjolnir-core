# Maps no longer draw black without ray tracing

**Date:** 2026-11-27
**Summary:** Converted maps drew black or nearly black on graphics cards without hardware ray tracing, such as a GTX 1080 Ti. They are lit again.

### Fixed

In MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.21.11.

- **Black maps on cards without hardware ray tracing.** The converted
  maps' surfaces carry their own ambient light. Where the engine's global
  illumination cannot run, it darkened that light by the surfaces' ambient
  occlusion, and the maps drew black: Gemini, Battle Creek and others on a
  GTX 1080 Ti. The loader now turns that darkening off for converted maps.
  From the same spot on a GTX 1080 Ti, Battle Creek's average brightness
  went from 0.8 to 39 and Gemini's from 0.4 to 23.6. Cards with ray
  tracing look exactly as before.

### Known issue

- On cards without hardware ray tracing, your first-person weapon can still
  look dark indoors.
