# Team scores on the HUD

**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Summary:** Multiplayer gets a live score strip and a scoreboard split into Red and Blue teams in Capture the Flag, and the lobby and map screens are tidied up.

### Added

- **MJOLNIRHud 0.2.0** shows a score strip at the top of the screen: Red
  and Blue totals in team games, your score against the leader's in free
  for all, with the score to win. It hides while the full scoreboard is
  open.
- The Tab scoreboard splits team games into Red and Blue sections with
  team totals, holds up to 16 players, and lists players still waiting for
  a team under "Awaiting assignment". A gold YOU marks your own row.
- The HUD is ready for Team Slayer, which maps don't offer yet: it scores
  each kill for the killer's team, and points stay with the team that earned
  them when a player switches sides or leaves.

### Changed

- **MJOLNIRLobby 0.1.1** refines the multiplayer lobby and map select
  screens.
- **MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.1.2** will colour Spartans' armour by team in
  Team Slayer too, as it does in Capture the Flag.

Update the CE runtime from the launcher's Multiplayer page too: the new
scoreboard needs the runtime pack's updated screens (1.0.2).
