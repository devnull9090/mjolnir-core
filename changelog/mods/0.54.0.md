# Pelican Warfare: a new game mode on a new map

**Date:** 2026-11-21
**Summary:** Pelican Warfare is a team objective mode on its own two-kilometre map: ride a Pelican in, jump, parachute down and fight over three capture zones.

### Added

In MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.21.0, MJOLNIRHud 0.23.0 and MJOLNIRLobby 0.28.0. The
mode needs the Pelican Warfare map from the hub.

- **Pelican Warfare.** Two teams board Pelicans at their bases, fly in and
  jump. You freefall, open a parachute and steer to the ground. Three capture
  zones, A, B and C, score for the team that holds them; the first team to
  500 wins. Stand in a zone to capture it. An enemy in the zone stops the
  capture and the scoring.
- **Zone tiles under the score.** Each zone shows who owns it, whether it is
  being captured, neutralized or contested, how far the capture has gone, and
  which teams are standing in it. The zone you are standing in has a gold rim.
- **Captures in the kill feed.** "Red team captured zone B" appears for
  everyone when a zone changes hands.
- **Tactical map.** Press M for an overview of the whole map with your team,
  the vehicles and the zones. N zooms.
- **Flight instruments.** Speed, sink rate, heading and height above ground
  while you fall and glide.

### Changed

In MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.21.0.

- **The flashlight works in dark rooms on custom maps.** Maps lit with
  physical exposure were 13 stops brighter than the campaign, so the game's
  flashlight barely showed. It is now raised to match while such a map is
  loaded and put back when you leave.
- **Water reflections behind shoreline trees.** A level can tell the loader
  how thick on-screen objects are to a reflection, which removes bright
  columns in the water behind saplings.

### Fixed

In MJOLNIRForge 0.4.5: the shared widget-class helper matches the other mods'.
