# Flashlight no longer blinding

**Date:** 2026-11-27
**Summary:** On converted and custom maps the flashlight whited out the screen. It is now bright enough to see by in the dark without blowing out.

### Fixed

In MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.21.13.

- **Flashlight far too bright on converted and custom maps.** Since mods
  0.54.0 the loader raised the flashlight by every stop of a map's extra
  lighting, as if the campaign never adjusted its exposure in the dark. On
  Battle Creek that was 8 stops, which turned anything within a few metres
  into a white blob, the weapon included. It now raises it by 5 stops less,
  allowing for the map's own exposure setting: 3 stops on Battle Creek.
  The campaign's flashlight is unchanged.
