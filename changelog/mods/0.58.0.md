# Faster frames on slower PCs

**Date:** 2026-11-26
**Summary:** Grass now follows your Video settings, and maps with lots of identical scenery draw it with far fewer materials. Up to 2.4 ms faster per frame on a GTX 1080 Ti.

### Improved

In MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.21.10.

- **Grass follows your Video settings.** The loader read the engine's
  foliage setting, which stays at its highest whatever you pick in Video.
  Medium players were getting the densest grass. It now takes the lower of
  the engine's setting and your Geometry setting. On a GTX 1080 Ti at
  Medium, Blood Gulch went from 15.99 to 13.58 ms a frame from the same
  spot, and Death Island gained about 1 ms.
- **Shared scenery materials.** Every placed piece of scenery that asked for
  a material got its own copy, even when hundreds asked for the same one.
  Pelican Warfare's ~1,000 rocks drew with 1,010 materials; they now share
  8, and the rocks went from costing up to 1 ms a frame to almost nothing.
  Other maps save fewer, from a handful to 112 on Desolation.
  Pelican Warfare also loads faster: dressing the map took 4.9 s on a
  GTX 1080 Ti PC and now takes 1.3 s.

### Added

In MJOLNIRHud 0.25.5.

- **Benchmark: hide a group of objects.** `mjolnir_bench hide <name>` and
  `mjolnir_bench hideclass <class>` time a frame with matching objects
  hidden against the same frame with them shown, alternating within one
  run so a changing match affects both. The bench's settings line now also
  records the game's own Video settings.
