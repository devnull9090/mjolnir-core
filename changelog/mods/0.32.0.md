# Team Slayer, CTF in team colours, and Halo 2 maps in the map list

**Date:** 2026-10-24
**Summary:** Team Slayer, CTF flags and armour in team colours with CE's own waving flag cloth, and a map list with every hub map, Halo 2 included.

### Added

- **Every map in SELECT MAP** (MJOLNIRLobby 0.14.1). The host's map list
  now holds up to 80 maps: the installed ones, then the hub's maps this PC
  doesn't have, marked DOWNLOAD. Pick one and SELECT opens the download
  screen; once it installs, it is picked and ready to host.
- **Filters for the map list** (MJOLNIRLobby 0.14.1). Chips above the list
  narrow it by game (Halo CE or Halo 2), by source (official or community)
  and to installed maps only, with a count of the maps shown.
- **Halo 2 maps' moving parts** (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.9.0). Turning parts
  such as Zanzibar's wheel, Ascension's antennas and Foundation's fans ride
  the simulation's own machines, so their collision turns with them; their
  see-through parts follow the same machine, and their sounds play as they
  turn.

- **Team Slayer** (MJOLNIRLobby 0.14.1, MJOLNIRHud 0.11.4). Red against
  Blue on every map that offers Slayer: an enemy kill scores 1 for your
  team, a teammate kill takes 1 away and shows BETRAYAL, and the first team
  to the kill limit wins.
- **CE's own flag cloth** (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.9.0). CTF flags carry Halo
  CE's red cobra and blue eagle banners, waving on the pole and streaming
  out behind whoever carries them, instead of a bare pole.

### Changed

- **The lobby's no-maps hints** (MJOLNIRLobby 0.14.1) send you to the
  launcher's Multiplayer page to install maps.
- **Every Spartan wears Mk V in team games** (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.9.0). In
  CTF and Team Slayer the game itself dresses every player in Mk V Classic,
  whatever armour or coating they picked, so the team colour shows on all
  of them; Slayer keeps everyone's own armour.
- **CTF needs CE runtime 1.13.0** (MJOLNIRLobby 0.14.1). The lobby offers
  CTF only when the runtime's blue flag is installed: install or update
  multiplayer in the launcher.

### Fixed

- **CTF flags kept their colours** (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.9.0). A flag no
  longer turns the other team's colour when it is dropped near a stand,
  when both flags move at once, or on a client; each flag's colour now comes
  from the game itself.
- **Custom armour showed its own colours in team games**
  (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.9.0): plates, helmets and coatings stayed on top of
  the team colour.
- **The dropped-flag reset and the health-pack respawn** took 15 seconds
  instead of 30: game types are now written at the game's 60 ticks a
  second.
