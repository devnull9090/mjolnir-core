# Snow and night skies on converted maps

**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Summary:** Converted maps can bring their weather and a sky drawn from their fog, so Coldsnap snows under its navy night sky.

### Added

- **Weather on converted maps** (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.6.0). A Custom
  Edition map whose BSP has a snow system now snows, using the game's own
  falling snow. The snow follows your view. Coldsnap is the first map with
  it.
  - Snow still falls indoors for now.
- **Skies without a model** (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.6.0). In CE, a sky with no
  model is just its fog colour. Converted maps now draw that colour past
  the fog's opaque distance instead of an empty black sky. On Coldsnap that
  is a dark navy night.

Maps converted before this release are unchanged. None of the 19 classic
maps has weather or a sky like this.
