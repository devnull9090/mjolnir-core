# Multiplayer on the Xbox app (Game Pass)

**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Summary:** Classic maps, multiplayer and FIND GAMES now work on the Xbox app (Game Pass) version of the game, not only on Steam.

### Fixed

- **The Xbox app version is supported** (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.6.0,
  MJOLNIRLobby 0.13.0, MJOLNIRBlamConsole 0.2.2). On Game Pass, starting a
  classic map said "is not registered: this game build is not CU4", and
  joining a game said the online tick was not hooked.
  - The Xbox app runs its own build of the same game version. Its addresses
    are now known alongside Steam's.
  - Joining from FIND GAMES or a link goes through the Xbox app's own "join
    game" handler, the way an accepted Xbox invite does.
  - The Blam console works on both versions.
  - Public games hosted from the Xbox app are listed as Game Pass.
- On the Xbox app a fireteam holds 16 players, as on Steam.

### Also in this release

- **CE weather and model-less skies** (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.6.0). The
  level loader can draw a converted map's falling snow and its fog-coloured
  night sky, as on Coldsnap. Maps that use them arrive in later map updates.
