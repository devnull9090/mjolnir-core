# Base beacons pulse again

**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Summary:** The glowing beacons at the bases on Danger Canyon, Blood Gulch and the big outdoor maps pulse as they did in Halo: Combat Evolved instead of shining steadily.

### Fixed

- **MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.1.1** animates the glow of lights that classic maps
  drive with an object function: Danger Canyon's base beacons pulse once a
  second again, as do the beacons on Blood Gulch, Sidewinder, Ice Fields,
  Timberland, Death Island and Infinity. Install the updated maps from the
  launcher's Multiplayer page to get it.
