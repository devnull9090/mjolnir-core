# Blue team shows blue, whatever armour you wear

**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Summary:** In CTF and Team Slayer, every Spartan now wears the classic Mk V armour in its team's colour. Players in other armour, like Master Chief's default, stayed olive.

### Fixed

- **Team colours for every armour.** Only the classic Mk V armour can take a
  team colour; the others carry their colours in their textures. A player
  who had picked another armour, such as Master Chief's default, stayed
  olive on their team, on every screen including their own. In team games
  everyone now wears Mk V in red or blue, as in Halo: Combat Evolved. Free
  for all games keep your own armour.

### Changed

- **MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.1.5**: the fix.
