# Faster foliage and /bench

**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Summary:** Grass that was drawn across the whole map now culls, Low and Medium foliage drop the grass's wind, and `/bench` measures what a map's foliage costs on your PC and sends it to the MJOLNIR team.

### Added

- **`/bench`** (MJOLNIRHud 0.10.0). Type `/bench` in match chat, stay put
  somewhere safe for about a minute, and the game times the map with and
  without its foliage, its shadows and its wind at your camera. The result
  shows in your chat and goes to the MJOLNIR team with your resolution,
  quality settings and GPU, so we can see how the remastered maps run on
  real hardware. It needs the launcher signed in to the hub, and it waits
  until you have been on the map for two minutes, while it is still loading
  in.
- **`mjolnir_bench`** in the console does the same without sending, or A/Bs
  any console variable: `mjolnir_bench r.Shadow.Virtual.Enable 0`. Results
  go to `bench.log` in the MJOLNIRHud folder.
- **Settings in the hitch log.** Each map's heading in `hitches.log` now says
  the resolution drawn, window mode, every quality level, the GPU and the CPU.

### Fixed

- **Grass drawn across the whole map** (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.7.2). Some
  bands of grass never culled at any distance: on Death Island, 51,000 clumps
  of the heaviest grass. Lower foliage quality made it worse. Every band now
  stops at its distance.
- **A loop that cost 12 ms a second** on maps with water dry zones
  (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.7.2). It looked up an engine object ten times a
  second.

### Changed

- **No grass wind at Low and Medium foliage quality** (MJOLNIRLevelLoader
  0.7.2). Swaying grass was half of the grass's cost on the GPU. High and
  Epic still sway.
- **MJOLNIRLobby 0.13.4** sends `/bench` results to the hub.

### Notes

- Foliage is about a fifth of the frame on Death Island, even on an RTX 5090
  (68 fps at 4K, Epic). If the remastered maps run slow for you, set
  **Foliage** to Medium or Low in Video settings.
