# Match history for public games

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Summary:** Public matches now report their scoreboard and every kill, with where it happened, to mjolnircore.com/matches.

### Added

- **Match history.** When a public match ends, the host's game sends it to
  mjolnircore.com: the final scoreboard and every kill, death, spawn, medal
  and flag capture, in order, with where each player was standing. See every
  match at mjolnircore.com/matches, with a timeline and a map of where the
  kills and deaths happened.
- **Your matches on your profile.** Your own game claims your seat in each
  public match you play, so your hub profile lists your matches, wins, kills
  and deaths. Players can also be looked up by in-game name.
- Private games are not reported.

### Changed

- **MJOLNIRHud 0.4.0** records public matches on the host and claims your seat
  when you play in someone else's.
- **MJOLNIRLobby 0.7.0** sends them to the hub with your launcher sign-in.
