# Markers that read the game itself

**Date:** 2026-11-09
**Summary:** King of the Hill and Juggernaut now read every player's place straight from the game rather than from the character models on screen, at a fraction of the cost.

### Changed

In MJOLNIRHud 0.20.0 and MJOLNIRLobby 0.26.0.

- **Markers that know where everyone is.** Who holds the hill, who contests
  it, and where the Juggernaut's beacon stands are now read from the game's
  own simulation, not from the character models the HUD draws. In our tests,
  with two copies of the game on one PC, each copy's model for the other
  player sat about 2.5 m above where that player really was, and the
  markers followed the model. From the simulation, each player is placed
  within a couple of centimetres of where their own game has them, on the
  host and on the copy that joined. A player waiting to respawn neither
  holds nor contests the hill.
- **Cheaper to find.** Reading two players' places took 62 µs from the models
  and under 5 µs from the game. King and Juggernaut do it five times a
  second, and the beacon that follows the Juggernaut reads them every frame.
  With an older MJOLNIRLobby the markers read the models as before.
