# Lit interiors and a flying camera

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Summary:** Remastered maps can now light their interiors with real bounced light, cliffs lose their black bands and hairlines, and FlyCam flies smoothly between saved camera shots.

### Added

In MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.13.15 and MJOLNIRFlyCam 1.3.0.

- **Light that bounces indoors.** A remastered map can now draw its level
  in pieces Unreal's Lumen can light. Rooms get real bounced light, shade in
  their corners and falloff down their stairwells instead of an even glow.
  Zanzibar and Coagulation are the first to use it.
- **Halo 2's own lights.** A Halo 2 map's placed lights now come through as
  real lights: Coagulation's bases are lit in their team colours again.
- **New lighting settings for map makers.** `sky_leak` lets a little sky
  light into enclosed spaces, and `lumen_cells` lists a map's lit pieces
  (see the level format docs).
- **FlyCam flies between shots.** Page Up and Page Down fly smoothly from one
  saved shot to the next. Shots now have their own panel with undo and redo,
  and FlyCam shows your real Spartan instead of a grey stand-in.

### Fixed

- **No more black band under cliff tops.** The soil drawn under a cliff's lip
  read as solid black on every remastered map; it is packed earth now.
- **No more hairline down tall rocks.** A thin dark line ran straight down
  some boulders and walls where the ground above them stepped.
- **Glass you can see.** Breakable windows that blocked you and shattered
  but could not be seen now draw again.
