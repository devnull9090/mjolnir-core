# King of the Hill and Oddball rules

**Date:** 2026-11-04
**Summary:** The rules and HUD for King of the Hill and Oddball on converted maps. A map offers them once its next release adds the markers.

### Added

- **King of the Hill and Oddball rules** (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.10.0). The
  loader ships both game types. The lobby offers one only on a map that lists
  it, and maps start listing them with their next releases.
- **Hill line** (MJOLNIRHud 0.15.0). In King of the Hill, a line under the score
  says how far the hill is and which way: "HILL 12 M LEFT ABOVE" or "ON THE
  HILL". Hosts relay each move of the hill to fireteam clients.
