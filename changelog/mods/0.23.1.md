# Joining games from the Xbox app

**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Summary:** On the Xbox app (Game Pass), choosing a game in FIND GAMES now joins it. Before, nothing happened and the join timed out.

### Fixed

- **Joining from the Xbox app** (MJOLNIRLobby 0.13.1). On Game Pass, a join
  from FIND GAMES or a link reached the game's invite handler and then went
  nowhere, so the player stayed in their own fireteam until the join gave
  up. The Xbox app raised the join where nothing in the game listens, and
  under a name the join does not read. The join now goes where the game
  listens, under the name it reads, and runs as it does on Steam: the game
  leaves its fireteam and joins the host's.

### Known issues

- A Steam player joining a game hosted on the Xbox app can be dropped a
  few seconds after connecting. The game refuses players from another
  platform when crossplay is off for either player, and on Xbox that
  follows the account's "You can join cross-network play" privacy setting.
  Check that setting if it happens.
