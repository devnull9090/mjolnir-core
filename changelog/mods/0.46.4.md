# The reason stays on screen

**Date:** 2026-11-07
**Summary:** A player the host starts without, or kicks from the post-game vote, now always sees why and the map's download, with no stray vote over it.

### Fixed

In MJOLNIRLobby 0.23.1.

- **The reason could vanish.** When the host started a map you don't have, or
  sent you back from the post-game vote, you left the fireteam and the game
  loaded the main menu again. If that reload came late, the screen telling you
  why, with the map's download, went with it, and you saw only the main menu.
  It now waits for the fresh main menu before it appears.
- **A vote of your own on the match just played.** Back on your own menu soon
  after a match you joined, your game could open a post-game vote of its own
  on that match and put it over the reason you were sent back. Only the
  host's vote is shown now.
