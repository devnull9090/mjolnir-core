# Back on your menu, and why

**Date:** 2026-11-07
**Summary:** When the host quits a match, joiners are back on their own menu in seconds with why; a kicked player always sees the reason and the map's download.

### Fixed

In MJOLNIRLobby 0.23.1.

- **Left on a black screen when the host quit.** When the host left a match
  with SAVE AND EXIT, everyone who had joined followed them into an empty
  menu: a black screen for about two minutes, then CONNECTION LOST. Now your
  game notices in about ten seconds and takes the same way out at once:
  CONNECTION LOST, and behind it "The host left the match." You're in a
  fireteam of your own again, as after the long wait. To bring everyone back
  to the lobby together, the host can still use RETURN TO LOBBY in the HOST
  MENU.
- **A vote on a match already played.** Back on your own menu after a match
  you joined, you could get a post-game vote of your own on it, which hid why
  you were there. Only the host's vote is shown now.
- **The reason could vanish.** When the host started a map you don't have, or
  sent you back from the post-game vote, you left the fireteam and the game
  loaded the main menu again. If that reload came late, the screen telling you
  why, with the map's download, went with it, and you saw only the main menu.
  It now waits for the fresh main menu before it appears.
