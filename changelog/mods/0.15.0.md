# Classic maps: baked corners, moving machines, truer shadows

**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Summary:** The level loader draws the 1.1.0 classic maps' new lighting: shadows stay out from under roofs, Infinity's beams fire, and a missing texture no longer blanks a material.

### Added

- **MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.2.0**: a level's lightmap shadow and sun levels
  (`lightmap_sun`) keep player and vehicle shadows to ground that was in the
  sun in Halo CE, so a Banshee on a base roof no longer shadows the room
  below it. Pairs with the 1.1.0 map packs, which also bake soft corner
  shading into every map.
- **Moving machines.** Infinity's beam emitters fire again: a machine's moving
  part is drawn by its material, and the loader widens the mesh's bounds so
  the beam is not culled as it climbs into the sky.

### Fixed

- **A texture missing from a map pack costs that texture, not the whole
  material.** Infinity's rocks and leaves drew flat grey because two
  multipurpose maps were missing from its pack; they now draw without them,
  and without the chrome-like reflection a missing multipurpose map caused.

### Notes

- `mjolnir_terrain_shadows` is a console command for comparing the terrain
  shadow modes (`on`, `off`, `lightmap`, `proxy`, `ao`) while a converted
  map is loaded.
