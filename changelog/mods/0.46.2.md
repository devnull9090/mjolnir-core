# Everyone where they really are

**Date:** 2026-11-07
**Summary:** The HUD now finds other players where they actually stand, so hill, Juggernaut and flag markers and the scoreboard agree on every PC in a match, the host's and a joiner's alike.

### Fixed

All in MJOLNIRHud 0.17.2.

- **A contested hill never showed.** With two players on the hill, both HUDs
  still said ON THE HILL while the game had stopped scoring. HILL CONTESTED now
  shows, and so does the colour of a hill someone else holds.
- **Markers pointed at the middle of the map.** A Juggernaut, Reverse Tag or
  Stalker marker for another player sat at the map's centre, or didn't show at
  all for someone who joined. It now follows that player.
- **A joiner's scoreboard didn't know the teams.** Someone who joined saw the
  host under AWAITING ASSIGNMENT and, for their first life, had no team of their
  own: no "/ YOU" and no Assault ATTACK or DEFEND line. Every team now shows from
  the start.
- **A second flag in Assault.** After a capture, a joiner could keep a ghost of
  the old flag, which drew a second marker and stopped the sides-swap clock at
  0:00. Only the real flag shows now.
- **A negative distance to the hill.** Standing under the hill, on another
  floor, read "-8 M ABOVE". It now reads 0 M, since you are already inside its
  circle and only need to go up or down.
