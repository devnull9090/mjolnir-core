# Death Island no longer washed out

**Date:** 2026-11-27
**Summary:** At the Low and Medium video settings, Death Island's sand, grass and rocks drew almost white after mods 0.59.1. They are lit normally again.

### Fixed

In MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.21.14.

- **Washed-out maps at Low and Medium settings.** Mods 0.59.1 made the sky
  light capture the sky again once a map is ready, so weapons are lit on
  cards without ray tracing. On maps whose Halo sky has a very bright
  horizon, that capture took in the sky dome itself and lit the ground far
  too strongly: Death Island's beach drew white. The Halo sky dome now
  stays out of that capture on every map. Weapons stay lit.
