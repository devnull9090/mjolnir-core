# Pelican Warfare: reinforcement waves, the Covenant, and a smoother game

**Date:** 2026-11-25
**Summary:** Pelican Warfare gains reinforcement waves, moving zones and Covenant drops, and every mode stutters far less on PCs running Windows Defender.

### Added

In MJOLNIRHud 0.25.4, MJOLNIRLobby 0.34.4 and MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.21.8. All three, the Pelican Warfare game type they ship and Pelican Warfare map 0.1.2 belong together: update the map in the launcher too.

- **A warmup countdown.** The line under the score says how long until
  deployment ("DEPLOYMENT IN 0:23"), or that the match is waiting for players.
  The last ten seconds count down in large numbers in the middle of the
  screen, gold for the last three, then "DEPLOY" as the Pelicans board.
  Everyone in the match sees the same number.
- **Reinforcement waves.** When you die you respawn at your base as before.
  Your team's Pelican waits low at the map's edge behind your base for half
  of each minute, and flies in just before it leaves. If you respawn then,
  you are put aboard straight away; otherwise you wait at base, shielded and
  unarmed, until it is back. On the minute it flies toward the middle of the
  map with everyone aboard. Jump when you like, or it puts you out near the
  centre, between A, B and C. The line under the score counts down to the
  next departure ("REINFORCEMENTS DEPART IN 0:23").
- **Watch a teammate while you wait.** While your Pelican waits, the view
  follows a teammate on the ground from behind, and the line says whom; with
  nobody to watch, it shows the zones from above.
- **Staying at base.** Press jump while the Pelican waits and you leave at
  once and parachute down near your base, to take a vehicle or go on foot.
  Walk 2 m or get in a vehicle and the Pelican leaves you alone.
- **Your parachute is yours.** It no longer opens by itself. Meet the ground
  too fast and you die.
- **Zones that move.** Each match picks where A, B and C are from several
  spots per zone, all on open ground. A and C always move together as mirror
  images, so neither team gets the nearer zones. The new places show from the
  moment the Pelicans launch, so you can steer your parachute to them.
- **The Covenant drop in.** 45 seconds into the match and every 90 seconds
  after, a Covenant Spirit flies in from the map's edge to the zone with the
  fewest Covenant, drops up to four squads and leaves. They fight both teams.
- **The Covenant take zones.** While a Covenant soldier is alive in a zone,
  nobody can capture it and it scores for no one. Left alone there, they turn
  your zone neutral and then take it for themselves: it goes purple, the feed
  says so, and it scores for no one until a team clears it and takes it back.
- **The Covenant can win.** If they hold A, B and C for a minute, the round
  ends and both teams lose. The HUD warns you how long is left.
- **A match clock.** During combat the line under the score shows the match
  time.
- **Slower captures.** A neutral zone now takes 15 seconds to capture (it was
  8), so an enemy's zone takes 30.
- **When the zones open.** During the insertion the line under the score
  counts down to when the zones open ("ZONES OPEN IN 0:23"), and the last ten
  seconds count down large in the middle of the screen, then "CAPTURE".
- **Zones in the top left.** A, B and C moved from the tiles under the score
  to three slim bars stacked in the top left corner: a hexagon in the owner's
  colour, what is happening there, and the capture bar.

### Changed

- **Far fewer stutters on PCs running Windows Defender.** Our mods handed a
  lot of information to each other through small files, and with Defender's
  real-time scan on (as Windows has it by default) reading a file that was
  just written waits about 5 ms. Those handoffs now go through memory. On our
  test PC our scripts' time on the game thread dropped from 31-57 ms of every
  second to 3-6 ms, and the slowest frames came down by 6 to 12 ms. A full
  Pelican Warfare match had 3 stutters during play instead of 15.
- **Announcer lines take turns.** A grenade into a squad used to start five
  or six multi-kill and spree lines at once. Now a burst plays its best line
  and the rest wait their turn or drop. This is in every mode.

### Fixed

- **Hitches when a match starts or you join.** Team colours (104 ms at a
  Blood Gulch start, 55-63 ms on joining Pelican Warfare), the map's level
  file read twice on joining (92 ms), the HUD's first moments in a match
  (60 ms), and a zone's walls drawn all at once (up to 30 ms) are each down to
  a few milliseconds a frame.
- **The post-game vote screen stuttered** every 1.5 seconds while it looked
  for menus that were hidden.
- **A kill-feed line could stutter** for 26 ms after a player's picture had
  been unloaded.
- **The Pelican launch stuttered** as all three zones' walls went up in the
  same frame.
- **A respawn's parachute** could fall back to the old canopy mesh.

MJOLNIRBridge 0.2.0, the remote control tool for testing, answers through a
named pipe instead of files.
