# Pelican Warfare: a warmup countdown and reinforcement waves

**Date:** 2026-11-22
**Summary:** Pelican Warfare counts the warmup down in big numbers, the dead come back together on a Pelican every minute while watching a teammate, and the capture zones can move from match to match.

### Added

In MJOLNIRHud 0.24.0, MJOLNIRLobby 0.32.0 and MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.21.1. All three, and the Pelican Warfare game type they ship, belong together: an older HUD or game type does not run the new mode, and the reverse.

- **A warmup countdown.** The line under the score says how long until
  deployment ("DEPLOYMENT IN 0:23"), or that the match is waiting for players.
  The last ten seconds count down in large numbers in the middle of the
  screen, in the game's own countdown style, gold for the last three, then
  "DEPLOY" as the Pelicans board. Everyone in the match sees the same number.
- **Reinforcement waves.** When you die you respawn at your base as before.
  Your team's Pelican waits near the base for half of each minute (with the
  next Pelican Warfare map, low at the map's edge behind your base, flying in
  just before it leaves);
  if you respawn then, you are put aboard straight away, and otherwise as
  soon as it is back. On the minute it flies the route toward the middle of
  the map with everyone aboard. Jump when you like, or it puts you out near
  the centre, between A, B and C. The line under the score counts down to the
  next departure ("REINFORCEMENTS DEPART IN 0:23").
- **Watch a teammate while you wait.** While your Pelican waits, the view
  follows a teammate on the ground from behind, and the line says whom. It
  comes back to your seat a few seconds before the wave leaves.
- **Staying at base.** Press jump while the Pelican waits and you leave at
  once and parachute down near your base, to take a vehicle or go on foot.
  Once you have walked or driven away from where you spawned, the Pelican
  leaves you alone.
- **Zones that move.** With the next Pelican Warfare map, each match picks
  where A, B and C are from several spots per zone, all on open ground. A and
  C always move together as mirror images, so neither team gets the nearer
  zones. The new places show from the moment the Pelicans launch, so you can
  steer your parachute to them.
- **The Covenant drop in.** With the next Pelican Warfare map, 45 seconds
  into the match and every 90 seconds after, a Covenant Spirit flies in from
  the map's edge to the zone with the fewest Covenant, drops up to four
  squads and leaves. They fight both teams.
- **The Covenant hold zones.** While a Covenant soldier is alive in a zone,
  nobody can capture it and it scores for no one. Clear them out first.
