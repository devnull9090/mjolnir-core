# /bench shows where the frame goes

**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Summary:** `/bench` now says whether your game thread, render thread or GPU is holding the frame back, and where you were standing, so the MJOLNIR team can tell what to optimize for your hardware.

### Changed

- **`/bench` measures where each frame's time goes** (MJOLNIRHud 0.11.0).
  Each part of the bench (with foliage, without it, without its shadows, without its
  wind) now records the game thread, render thread and GPU times that `stat
  unit` shows. A saving on the GPU does nothing while your CPU is the slow
  part, so this tells us which part a fix has to target. Your chat
  shows the split for your normal frame.
- **`/bench` sends the camera's position.** Two benches on one map can read
  very differently depending on where you stand. The report now says where
  that was.
- **`mjolnir_bench`** prints the same split on every row of `bench.log`.

### Notes

- The split comes from a small native file in the MJOLNIRHud folder,
  `native\mjolnir_frame_times.dll`. If a game update moves what it reads, the
  bench still runs and just leaves the split out.
