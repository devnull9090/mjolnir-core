# The parachute's hands stay on the handles

**Date:** 2026-11-21
**Summary:** In Pelican Warfare, opening the parachute while looking down no longer leaves your arms in front of your face.

### Fixed

In MJOLNIRHud 0.23.1.

- **Parachute arms in the right place.** Your hands take the two steering
  handles when the parachute opens. They were placed from where your arms
  were at that moment, so opening it while looking down at the ground left
  two large forearms across the view for the whole glide. They now sit on
  the harness at eye height, wherever you were looking.
