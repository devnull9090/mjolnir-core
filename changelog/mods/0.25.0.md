# A new kill feed

**Date:** 2026-10-17
**Summary:** The multiplayer kill feed shows each weapon's icon, headshots and players' avatars, with medals, score bars and a new respawn countdown.

### Added

- **An animated kill feed** (MJOLNIRHud 0.7.0). Each kill is a row: the
  killer and the victim with their avatars, and between them the weapon,
  drawn as the game's own HUD icon. Headshots, splatters, stuck grenades and
  assassinations get their own icon. Joins, quits, kicks, falls, flag
  captures and medals get rows too. Rows slide in, rise as new ones arrive
  and fade out. A row that names you has a gold edge, and team kills say
  BETRAYAL.
- **Medals.** Double Kill through Killionaire, the killing sprees and First
  Blood appear in the feed. Your own also come up in the middle of the
  screen.
- **Score bars.** The score at the top of the screen is two bars that fill
  toward the score to win, with the round clock between them.
- **A bigger respawn countdown.**
- The feed, the score bars and the countdown use the game's own fonts.
- `mjolnir_feed_demo` (console, in a match) previews the feed, medals and
  score bars.

### Fixed

- **Players who joined someone else's match** saw few kills in the feed
  and on the scoreboard. The host now sends them every kill, death, medal
  and capture.
- **The scoreboard's avatars** no longer cost the game time each time the
  scoreboard redraws.

Needs CE runtime 1.9.0 for the new look. With an older runtime, the feed
is plain text lines.
