# Smoother frames on the classic maps

**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Summary:** The occasional stutter on the classic maps, a frame of 50 to 200 ms every minute or two even standing still, is gone. It came from MJOLNIR's own scripts cleaning up their memory, not from the maps.

### Fixed

- **Stutters while standing still** (MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.7.3). Every
  minute or two the level script's memory cleanup ran in one go and held a
  frame for 30 to 200 ms. The cleanup now runs a little at a time, and the
  script makes far less to clean up. The flashing beacon lights on Death
  Island alone made about 200 KB of it a second, and every rock placed on
  the cliffs left a little behind that was never freed. On Death Island no
  script now holds a frame for more than 3 ms, and the worst frame in a
  five-minute test was 17 ms.
- **A short HUD hitch every 15 seconds** (MJOLNIRHud 0.11.1). The kill feed
  and scoreboard script's own cleanup held a frame for 4 to 9 ms. It now
  holds one for at most 2 ms.
