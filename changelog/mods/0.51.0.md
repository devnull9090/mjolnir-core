# Every medal

**Date:** 2026-11-10
**Summary:** Multiplayer now shows every medal the game awards (headshots, revenge, hijacks, weapon sprees and more), and the medal case counts them without doubles.

### Added

In MJOLNIRHud 0.21.0 and MJOLNIRLobby 0.27.0.

- **All 100 medals.** The game already awarded Reach's whole set of
  medals; the HUD showed only first blood, multi-kills and killing sprees.
  Now it shows them all: headshots and sniper headshots, Pummel, Beat Down
  and Assassination, Revenge, Killjoy, Opportunist, Close Call, From the
  Grave, Avenger, Lifesaver, Hijack and Skyjack, Splatter and Stuck, the
  weapon sprees (Sniper Spree, Shotgun Spree, Splatter Spree and their
  bigger brothers), the objective medals, and multi-kills and sprees past
  the old cap.
- **Big moments in the feed, the rest on your screen.** Multi-kills, sprees,
  weapon sprees, objective medals and First Blood get a feed line for
  everyone. Medals about how a kill was made (a headshot, a melee, a
  revenge) come up only in the middle of the earner's own screen, so the
  feed does not grow a second line for every kill. One kill's medals come
  up best first, the top three of a burst.
- **No doubles.** The game sends a plain headshot along with every sniper
  headshot. Only the sniper headshot is shown now, and a wheelman spree no
  longer also shows the assist spree it stands for.
- **Fireteam players see them too.** The host sends each medal, already
  sorted out, after the kill lines it belongs to, and a busy moment is
  split over several messages instead of losing its last lines.

- **Medals after the match.** The post-game screen says which medals you
  earned and who earned the most, under the winner. On the hub, a match's
  page has a Medals column on its scoreboard and each player's medals by
  kind underneath.

### Changed

- **Medal case on the hub.** Hijacks are counted (ten were recorded and
  never counted), a headshot that came with a sniper headshot counts once,
  and the case has new medals: Pummel, Opportunist, Speed Kill, Avenger,
  Lifesaver, Supercombine, Killtacular, Rampage, Sharpshooter, Open Season,
  Splatter Spree, Flag Carrier Kill, Extermination and Perfection. Match
  timelines name the rarer medals too.

### Under the hood

- Which incidents are medals, what each is called, which one hides another
  and their order are decided in C in MJOLNIRLobby's DLL
  (`native/lobby/medals_core.h`, tested by `test_medals.c` in CI). The HUD's
  Lua keeps only its incident hook, the drawing and the relay message, and
  its own medal table is gone. With an older MJOLNIRLobby there are no
  medals, and the HUD log says why.
- The research behind it, with every medal and what raises it:
  `docs/re/medals.md`.
