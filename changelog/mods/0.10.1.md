# Classic CE maps run smoothly

**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Summary:** Converted Halo CE maps no longer stutter. The mods were stalling the game many times a second, whatever the graphics settings or the PC.

### Fixed

- **Stutter on converted maps.** Blood Gulch and the other classic maps
  hitched several times a second and could be close to unplayable, even on
  fast PCs at low settings. The mods kept searching through every object in
  the game (about 20 ms each time on a converted map) to find the player.
  They now ask the engine directly. On Blood Gulch the worst frame went from
  265 ms to 9 ms, and the average from 59 to 190 frames a second.
- **A freeze every second and a half in a match.** The multiplayer lobby kept
  checking for the main menu during matches. It now waits until you are back
  at the menu.

### Changed

- **MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.1.4**, **MJOLNIRHud 0.3.1**, **MJOLNIRLobby 0.3.1**,
  **MJOLNIRCTF 0.1.1**, **MJOLNIRWorldBuilder 0.1.2**, **MJOLNIRBridge 0.1.2**
  and **MJOLNIRMapMenu 0.1.2**: the stutter fix.
