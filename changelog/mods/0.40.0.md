# Bots on red and blue

**Date:** 2026-11-01
**Summary:** In team games bots play for red and blue and leave their own side alone. Bots no longer stand still for minutes, and the post-game vote shows each option as a tile.

### Added

- **Bots play for red and blue in team games** (MJOLNIRHud 0.14.2,
  MJOLNIRLobby 0.19.0). Each bot joins the side with fewer players. It
  leaves players of its own side alone and fights the other side's bots and
  players. Kills between bots reach the kill feed, the scoreboard and the
  team score, and a player who kills a bot of their own side is counted a
  betrayal.
- **Bots come in where their side is** (MJOLNIRHud 0.14.2), away from
  enemies and never on top of anyone, as the game's own respawns bring
  players in. This needs a map released after this one (it ships the map's
  `bots.json`); on an older map each bot keeps its fixed start.
- **The post-game vote as tiles** (MJOLNIRLobby 0.19.0): each option shows
  its map's screenshot, the game type's icon, its votes and its share of
  them, with a countdown in the header. The winner is ringed in gold as
  NEXT GAME. The tiles need the next CE runtime pack; with the current one
  the vote looks as before.

### Fixed

- **Bots that stood still for minutes** (MJOLNIRHud 0.14.2). A bot's body
  could stay asleep while its AI wanted to move, so it stood and took fire
  without turning. Every order now wakes it. A bot that is still stuck after
  a minute is placed again.
- **Bots hunted the wrong player** (MJOLNIRHud 0.14.2) when the seats in use
  had gaps.
- **Bots in a team game no longer shoot players of their own side**
  (MJOLNIRLobby 0.19.0).

### Known issues

- Elites can jam their heads under the lintels of Blood Gulch's side doors.
- Bots leaving Blood Gulch's flag rooms, bots on Night Lockout, and bots
  spreading over the whole map need the maps rebaked with this release's
  tools and released again.
