# Containment crash fix

**Date:** 2026-11-18
**Summary:** Fixes a crash loading Containment, most often after playing Zanzibar.

### Fixed

In MJOLNIRLevelLoader 0.20.1.

- **No crash on Containment.** The game could crash a couple of seconds
  after Containment loaded, right after Zanzibar or whenever its gates had
  not finished loading. The loader now waits for a moving part to load
  before it dresses it. Containment 2.0.1 and Ascension 2.0.1, out now,
  also bring back Containment's gates and panels.
