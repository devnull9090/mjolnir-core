# AI in a Megalo (multiplayer) game

Issue #247 (bots to fill lobbies), route B: can the simulation's own AI play in
a Slayer match? Spike run 2026-10-25 on CU4, one PC, host only.

## The test map

`examples/levels/bot_b40_beach.level.json`, baked as the standalone codename
`BOT`:

```bash
mjolnir level bake examples/levels/bot_b40_beach.level.json --standalone BOT --install-test
```

It is B40 (The Silent Cartographer) under the Megalo engine on B40's **stock**
BSP 8 (`bsp_01_1_start`, the Forerunner interior at insertion point 0), with
the mission's squads, squad groups, zones, AI objectives, character palette and
`ai pathfinding data` kept (`clear.squads: false`, none of those in
`clear.blocks`). No `single_bsp`: squad spawn points name their BSP by index
(`structure bsp #8`) and the single-BSP pass does not remap them. Four player
starts and `player_spawn` scenery sit on B40's own insertion-0 starts.
Only four squads stand on BSP 8: `insertion_cov_grunts_flee`,
`insertion_cov_grunts_interior`, `insertion_cov_elites`,
`pelican_arrival_squad`.

Hosted with `mjolnir_auto host BOT slayer private`. AI is placed with
`mjolnir_sim_script` lines (MJOLNIRLobby's DLL, sim thread), never with the
Blam console from Lua mid-game.

The script path reports nothing back, so results were read with colour fades:
`(if (> (ai_living_count insertion_cov_elites) 0) (fade_out 0 1 0 5) (fade_out 0 0 1 5))`
turns the screen green or blue.

## Findings

1. **`ai_place` is a no-op in a multiplayer game.** Its core
   (`sim+0xfd810`, called from the `ai_place` evaluator `sim+0x1c0240`) runs
   only when the game options' mode byte (`[TLS+0x60]+0x10`, the same options
   block whose `+0x15f0` holds the live variant) is 1 (campaign) or 2
   (Firefight, with options `+0` or `+1` set). A Megalo match is mode 3, so
   the squad is never placed. `ai_enabled` is true throughout.

   ```text
   sim+0xfd839  80 78 10 01   cmp byte [rax+10h], 1
   sim+0xfd83d  74 22         jz  +0x22   ; campaign: place
   ...                                    ; else mode 2 check, else return
   ```

   Patching `sim+0xfd83d` `74 22` → `eb 22` (always place) makes `ai_place`
   work in the Megalo game. No other gate was needed for what follows.

2. **Placed AI think, path and fight under Megalo.** Elites and Grunts spawned
   at their squad spawn points, walked tens of metres through the BSP towards
   the player (pathfinding from the kept `ai pathfinding data`), shot, and
   killed the player. The Pelican of `pelican_arrival_squad` flew in. Placing
   the same squad again adds its members again.

3. **Incidents carry AI kills both ways**, through the same
   `BPC_MeteoriteIncidentHandlerComponent:OnIncident_Event` the HUD hooks:

   | Event | Cause | Effect |
   |---|---|---|
   | AI kills the player: `death` (no `Kill`) | index −1, `CauseObjectActor` = `BP_GruntBipedActor_C`, damage `PlasmaPistol` | index 0, `BP_SpartansBipedActor_C` |
   | player kills AI: `Kill`, `auto_kill`, `kill_elite` / `kill_grunt`, `kill_enemy_leader`, `headshot_kill` ... | index 0, the player's biped, damage `AssaultRifle` | index −1, `BP_EliteBipedActor_C` / `BP_GruntBipedActor_C` |

   Megalo respawned the dead player after the variant's 5 s, with the usual
   `respawn_tick` / `player_spawn` incidents.

So a bot's kills and deaths can be told apart from players' by player index
−1 and recognised by their biped actor. A scoreboard row for a bot is
bookkeeping in the HUD's match model, keyed by the AI biped, with no sim work.

## Since the spike

Bots now play on Blood Gulch, host and joiner ([../bots.md](../bots.md)).
Answered since:

- **Fireteam clients** see the host's AI move and fight (two PCs,
  2026-10-25): the simulation replicates them; positions of AI far from the
  joiner's player lag a few metres.
- **Converted maps' pathfinding** is generated by the bake
  (`blam_sbsp::pathfinding`). The format, read off B40: `sectors` (8 bytes:
  flags, hint, first link), `links` (16: vertex 1, vertex 2, flags, hint,
  forward, reverse, left sector, right sector; forward continues the left
  sector's ring, reverse the right's), `bsp2d refs` one per collision surface
  of the BSP or object (a sector index, `0x80000000 | node` into `bsp2d
  nodes`, or -1), `vertices`. B40's own sectors all lie on instanced geometry
  (its `object refs`, one per definition), and its BSPs set `no default
  structure pathfinding`; a converted map's shell carries the sectors, so the
  bake clears that flag.
- **A scenario with AI needs `ai user hint data[0]`.** `sim+0x16d020` reads the
  block's first element without checking the count (AV at 0x48 on Blood
  Gulch, whose bake had cleared the block). The bots lane keeps one element,
  every hint list emptied.

## Steps and seams in Bungie's pathfinding

Read off the shipped scenarios with `pf_dump <scenario> <sbsp> <bsp> raw`
(and `risers`, `shell` for a converted shell) on 2026-10-30, to let bots
cross the lips and cracks of converted maps (`blam_sbsp::pathfinding`).

**Flag names** come from the tag definitions (`defs/hce/tag-definitions.json`).
Sector flags: walkable 0x1, breakable 0x2, mobile 0x4, bsp source 0x8, floor
0x10, ceiling 0x20, wall north 0x40, south 0x80, east 0x100, west 0x200,
crouchable 0x400, aligned 0x800, step 0x1000, interior, rail, user. Link
flags: from collision edge 0x1, intersection link 0x2, bsp2d creation error
0x4, topology error 0x8, chain error 0x10, threshold 0x20, wall base 0x40,
ledge 0x80, leanable 0x100 (low 0x200, high 0x400), start corner 0x800, end
corner 0x1000, seam 0x2000.

**Steps.** Across B40, A30, A50, B30, C10 and D20 (about 150,000 sectors)
the only sectors that are not `walkable | floor` (0x11, or 0x15 on objects)
are steps: `walkable | step | wall <one direction>`, no floor bit. A short
riser is a sector of its own, linked to the floor above and below through
its edges (flags none or threshold). Most are on one object B40 repeats in
every BSP (rises 0.007 to 0.078 wu, sloped up to nz 0.49); four lie on
structure, pure vertical risers of 0.03 and 0.04 wu with normal (-1, 0, 0)
flagged `walkable | wall west | aligned | step` (0x1a01). So -x is west; the
generator takes +y as north. The shipped `ai_globals` say where a step ends:
`hoist step` is (0.1, 0.4) wu and `max jump down height down` 0.1, so a rise
under 0.1 wu is walked, anything taller hoisted or jumped.

**Intersection links** join sectors on separate geometry (every B40 instance
boundary; 194 hints in BSP 8 alone, all of type 0 `intersection link`):

- each sector keeps its own boundary link along the seam (`right sector`
  -1), flagged `intersection link` (0x2, most often alone) with a hint of its
  own: no hint serves two links;
- the hint is `(type 0, next, data (joining link, 0, 0, 0))`; about one in
  five carries `data1 = 1` and two more numbers (a piece and an index, it
  seems) that the zero form does without;
- both hints name one two-sided link, all of them after the regular links:
  its vertices are the first link's (`left sector` the first's sector), its
  `forward link` is the first link's, its `reverse link` the second link's
  `forward link`, flags 0 or threshold, no hint. It is in neither ring;
- a sector's `hint index` heads a chain (`next hint index`) of exactly the
  hints of its own ring's links (632 of 632 sectors in BSP 8), -1 without.

The B40 pieces' seams coincide exactly. Converted maps built of slabs leave
cracks: Night Lockout's floors end in their slab's own side face, with the
next slab's floor edge to edge, 0.01 wu inside or up to about 0.1 wu away.
The generator joins two floor edges that both drop away, run opposite ways
within 0.1 wu and lie less than a step apart in height, cutting each link at
the shared stretch's ends first so the joining link spans exactly it.

**Player starts** in the hub maps' level files stand 1.0 wu above their
floor (every start of Night Lockout, Blood Gulch and Boarding Action; with
the spawn lookup allowing 0.25 wu, no hub map loses a start).

## Open

- **Megalo score**: Slayer's kill trigger fires on a *player* killed, so AI
  kills likely do not score; a score-to-win end needs the HUD's own count or
  a variant change.
- **Looks**: no shipped Spartan AI character (`spartans_ai` ships only as a
  stimuli character). A `new-tag` clone of `trooper` pointed at the
  `spartans` biped is the likely route.
