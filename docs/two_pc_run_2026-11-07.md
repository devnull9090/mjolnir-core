# Two-PC run, 2026-11-07

PC 1 hosts and PC 2 (VENUS) joins, with nobody at either PC. Both run what the
hub ships: launcher 0.23.0, mods 0.46.1, CE runtime 1.19.0, each brought up to
date through its own launcher. This run covers what one PC can't prove: things
between two players, and what a joiner sees.

PC 2 joins through the lobby. PC 1 lists the game while PC 2 joins, with MAX
PLAYERS at 2 so nobody else can, then goes private before the match starts, so
no test match lands in hub history.

## The list

0. **Same files on both PCs.**
1. **Join and follow.** PC 2 joins PC 1's lobby and follows it into Blood Gulch
   Slayer. Both simulations hold both players, and both boards show 2.
2. **Game types between two players**, each checked on both screens:
   - Team King (Blood Gulch): teams, hill colour, contested, score bar.
   - King and Crazy King (Prisoner): one player scores, two contest.
   - Juggernaut (Blood Gulch): a kill makes the Juggernaut, killing them passes it on.
   - Oddball and Team Oddball (Prisoner): the carrier scores on both boards.
   - Reverse Tag and Stalker (Blood Gulch): roles pass by kills.
   - Race and Rally (Prisoner): each PC's own next flag, laps.
3. **Yoyorast Island with a joiner:** a client presses a gate button and both
   see the gate; Assault with one player on each side; Forge's map vehicles on
   the joiner's screen.
4. **Bots with a joiner:** Team Slayer with bots; both boards list them.
5. **End of a match:** score limit, both on the post-game screen, a vote, both
   travel to the next map.

   (Night-Lockout), and installs it in game first.
7. **Join in progress:** Crazy King, PC 2 joins a minute in and sees the same

## Results

### 0. Same files

The 24 hub items on both PCs have identical container hashes. PC 1 also has 7
maps PC 2 doesn't (Coldsnap, Night-Lockout, Lockout (Halo 2), Forge Canvas,
Roadworks, Roadworks Circuit, Proving Ground). PC 1 has 10 optional code mods

in one pass.

### 1. Join and follow: passes

PC 2 joined PC 1's listed lobby (`mjolnir_auto join`), PC 1 went private, and
both followed it into Blood Gulch Slayer. Both simulations hold both players
at the same spots. A kill each way scored on both screens: PC 1's YOU 1 with
PC 2's body at its feet, PC 2's YOU 0 / LEADER 1 after respawning.

Tools this needed (agent 11, `remote.mjs`):
- **Each PC owns its own player's position.** A host writing a joiner's
  biped snaps straight back, so PC 2's player is moved inside PC 2's game
  (`remote.mjs teleport`, `objects`).
- **`mjolnir live` refused a joiner's object table**: its header entries don't
  repeat their own slot (slot 0 read 4). Now only an index past the table's
  end is refused; on PC 2 the bipeds then matched its own Unreal actors.
- **PC 2's player is driven from inside the game** (`remote.mjs game-input`,
  native/test_input's DLL), so kills go both ways.

### 5. End of a match: the screen agrees; the vote found a test leftover

PC 1 held Prisoner's hill to 120. Both PCs logged the round over and showed the
same POST-GAME screen: DEVNULL WINS 120-0, the same four options, the same
countdown. Nobody voted, and the vote chose **Ivory Tower, Assault, a map
neither PC has**. The host went into the game's own campaign flow and stopped
on MISSION SELECT, and PC 2 stayed on STARTING THE NEXT GAME.

Cause: both PCs still had an `MJOLNIRLevelLoader\maps.json` from `mjolnir
level bake --install-test` (PC 1's dated 2026-11-06, PC 2's 2026-11-05),
which the cleanup missed. It registers 29 Halo 2 and test maps that aren't
installed, and the vote builds its options from that list. A player's install
never has the file. Both were moved into each PC's `MJOLNIR-backup-20261107`,
with ledger entries.

Still worth a guard: starting a map whose world isn't installed should fail
with a message, not strand the fireteam on MISSION SELECT. The vote should
also offer only maps whose containers are mounted.

### 3. Yoyorast Island with a joiner

**Assault: the simulation passes; three HUD bugs on the joiner, fixed.**
- Blue defended first: the flag stood on Blue's stand. PC 1 (Red) took it and
  scored at Red's stand. Red got 1 on both bars, and PC 1's line went from
  ATTACK / SIDES SWAP IN 4:34 to DEFEND / SIDES SWAP IN 4:42. Five minutes
  later the sides swapped by time: the flag went home to Blue's stand on both
  simulations.
- **The joiner's own team was unknown for its first life.** PC 2's bar read
  RED ... BLUE with no "/ YOU", and it never showed ATTACK or DEFEND. Its
  first spawn reaches the HUD only through the host's relay, which carries no
  biped. Team King's first screenshot on PC 2 had the same gap. Fix: the
  HUD reads its own team from its pawn's unit (`GetBlamObjectActor`).
- **A deleted flag's actor stays on the joiner, coloured.** After the capture
  PC 2 showed a second, blue marker at the stand just captured from. After the
  timed swap a red one stayed at Red's stand. Neither flag was carried or
  teleported in that second case. The host recycles the actor; the joiner keeps
  the old one where it last stood. Its object handle shares the new flag's
  slot with an older salt (`e3660094` beside the live `e36e0094`).
- **So the joiner's clock stopped at 0:00.** With the leftover red flag beside
  the live blue one, PC 2 still read Red as defending, so it never counted the
  swap. Fix (`flagmark.lua`): an actor whose slot holds a later object is a
  leftover, not a flag. Tested in `test_flag_marker.lua` with the handles above.

**Gates: a joiner's button press works.** PC 2 stood 1.4 m from the button by
Blue's base and saw Activate Device. A 500 ms E raised the gate at
(14.9, -4.8) on both PCs (position 0 to 1), and it dropped back on both 60 s
later. From 3 m there was no prompt, for the host or the joiner.

### 5, again: a vote with ballots

Both PCs voted for Crossline (Slayer). Both screens showed 2 OF 2 VOTED, 2 on
Crossline and YOUR VOTE on it, and both travelled into Crossline when the vote
ended. With no ballots at all, the vote also carried both to the game it chose
(Prisoner to Battle Creek).

**A tool lesson that cost two crashes of each PC:** `MJ_Event` takes a string
(`Name: StrProperty`). Calling it from the bridge with `FName("vote:1")`
crashed the game inside UE4SS. The first crash on PC 1 had `vote:1` and
`wbp_mjolnirpostgame_c_` on its stack. Pass a plain Lua string, and only to the
screen that `IsActivated()` and `IsVisible()`. `FindAllOf` also returns a class
pin that is neither.

PC 2 also crashed once 8 s into a King match on Blood Gulch, before any vote
call. Its dump didn't hold the crashing thread's stack, and its UE4SS frames
match the call crashes, so it wasn't proven. At the time, the HUD's team fix
called `GetBlamObjectActor()` on every pawn once a second, and for another
player's pawn that returns a pointer that is no object. That call is gone: the
team comes from the biped search instead. In the nine matches after it
(Assault, King, Reverse Tag, Night-Lockout, Boarding Action, Crazy King twice,
King, Crossline), the only crashes were from the `FName` vote calls.

## Fixed in this branch (MJOLNIRHud 0.17.2)

- `markers.lua`, `main.lua`: the hill and the Juggernaut, Reverse Tag and
  Stalker markers read other players from their biped, not their pawn. A
  contested hill now shows, another player's hill colour shows, and a remote
  Juggernaut's marker is on them. Proven both ways on two PCs.
- `main.lua`: on a joiner, a biped search, in King and Juggernaut types and in
  team games, while one is needed. It gives a joiner every team on its board,
  its own included (the "/ YOU" and Assault's ATTACK or DEFEND). The first
  version searched the whole object array each time, and one search held a
  joiner's frame 89 ms. Now the actors are found once a match and later ones
  arrive through `NotifyOnNewObject`, as in `bots.lua`. Over four Juggernaut
  passes on PC 2, the first held its frame 78 ms (the one search) and none of
  the rest more than 32 ms, with both PCs agreeing on every holder and
  position.
- `flagmark.lua`: a deleted flag's leftover actor on a joiner is no flag, so
  there's no phantom marker and Assault's role and clock follow the real flag.
- `hill.lua`: no negative distance under a hill.
- `mjolnir_markers` logs what the markers know, for the next two-PC test.

Tools: agent 11 (`objects`, `teleport`), `remote.mjs game-input`, and
`mjolnir live objects` on a joiner's table.
