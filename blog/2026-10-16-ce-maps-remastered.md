# Six classic maps, remastered in Campaign Evolved's look

**Author:** MJOLNIR Core
**Summary:** Blood Gulch, Death Island, Ice Fields, Sidewinder, Timberland and Yoyorast Island now look like they belong in Campaign Evolved: real terrain and grass, the game's own rock and trees, a living ocean and river, and Halo CE's lamps lit by Unreal Engine. Drag the pictures to compare.
**Tags:** multiplayer, maps, lighting

![Death Island's beach, remastered](/blog-images/ce-maps-remastered/death-island-beach.jpg?before=/blog-images/ce-maps-remastered/death-island-beach-classic.jpg)

The classic maps came to Halo Campaign Evolved looking like Halo CE, and
[lighting them with Unreal Engine](/blog/lighting-the-classics) kept them that
way: the same surfaces, the same colours, real light. This update goes
further on the six outdoor maps. Their ground, rock, grass, trees and water
are now drawn the way Campaign Evolved draws its own levels, while the
layout and every weapon, vehicle and flag spawn stay exactly where Halo CE
put them. The new rocks and tree trunks are solid where they stand, as Halo
CE's boulders and trees were.

Every comparison below is the current hub version of a map (left) against
the remastered one (right), from the same camera. Drag the divider to compare.

## What changed

- **Real terrain.** The ground under each map is rebuilt as an Unreal
  Engine landscape from Halo CE's own heights. Sand turns to earth and
  gravel inland, banks go green as they steepen, and the beaches get ripples
  you can see from eye level.
- **Grass that grows.** Several kinds of grass, weeds and tufts, at
  different heights, on the ground Halo CE painted green. Rocks and pebbles
  scatter where it painted sand. Nothing grows inside tunnels, where it
  would only stand in the dark.
- **Campaign Evolved's rock and trees.** Cliffs are drawn in the game's own
  layered rock instead of one tiled texture, with B30's cliff stones set into
  the walls. Halo CE's boulders and trees become the campaign's own, sized
  to match.
- **Water.** Death Island and Yoyorast sit in a real ocean that swells and
  breaks on the sand, over a sea floor that falls away from the shore.
  Timberland's stream is now a river that runs the length of the map and
  pours over its falls.
- **Tracks in the sand.** Drive a Warthog across Death Island's beach and it
  leaves tyre tracks, as the campaign's own beaches do. Footprints follow
  you too.
- **Halo CE's lamps, lit by Unreal.** Death Island's twenty light fixtures
  now cast real light and shadow inside its bases.
- **Snow.** Ice Fields and Sidewinder get snow-covered ground and rock, and
  falling snow.
- **Life.** Birds over the cliffs, gulls along the shore, falling leaves and
  wind.

## Getting it

The remastered maps arrive as an ordinary update to each map: update the
six maps in the MJOLNIR launcher. Update the launcher first. The remastered maps carry their grass
and decal files beside the level, and they need the latest CE runtime pack,
which the updated launcher installs for you.

The other thirteen classic maps are unchanged. They are indoor or built
maps, where there is little ground to remaster.
