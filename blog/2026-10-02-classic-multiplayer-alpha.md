# Classic Halo CE multiplayer comes to Campaign Evolved: the alpha

**Author:** MJOLNIR Core
**Summary:** All nineteen original Halo: Combat Evolved multiplayer maps, rebuilt to run inside Halo Campaign Evolved, with Slayer, Capture the Flag and your fireteam. Install them in one click from the launcher. Alpha testing starts now.
**Tags:** multiplayer, maps, alpha

Halo Campaign Evolved ships a campaign. It doesn't ship Blood Gulch.

Starting today it can. The MJOLNIR launcher installs all **nineteen original
Halo: Combat Evolved multiplayer maps**, rebuilt to run inside Campaign
Evolved, and a **MULTIPLAYER** menu to play them from with your fireteam. It's
an alpha: it works, it has rough edges, and we want you to find them.

![Blood Gulch in Halo Campaign Evolved](/api/v1/media/85842056-a669-4f26-845f-ade0ed2818cb)

## How to play

1. Update the launcher to **0.9.0** (it offers the update itself). If you
   haven't set up mods yet, its **My Mods** page does that first. Then open the
   new **Multiplayer** page.
2. Press **Install multiplayer**. The launcher downloads every map, the shared
   CE content they use, and the multiplayer mods, then registers the maps with
   your copy of the game. Press it again later to update.
3. Start the game and choose **MULTIPLAYER** on the main menu. Pick a map and a
   game type, press **INVITE FRIENDS** to bring people into your fireteam, and
   **START GAME**.

Your friends need the same maps installed, so send them the launcher too.

## What you get

- **Slayer and Capture the Flag** on every map, with team scoring and the
  announcer.
- **The maps as they were.** Each map keeps its own collision, so you walk
  where you walked in 2001: its spawns, its weapons and vehicles, its
  teleporters, health packs, grenades, overshields and active camo.
- **The look of 2001.** CE's own textures, lightmaps and skies, its water
  rippling and reflecting the sky, and the base beacons pulsing as they did.
- **A multiplayer HUD.** A kill feed that names the weapon, a respawn
  countdown, a scoreboard on **Tab** (the View button on a controller), and
  name tags over your teammates in team games, never over your enemies.
- **Screens of our own.** The lobby and map select are new menus cooked into
  the game, not buttons painted over the campaign's.

## How it works

Every map is converted from the original Halo CE map file into a new mission of
its own. Its terrain, textures, lightmaps, sky and sounds become Unreal assets.
Its collision becomes a Blam structure BSP the simulation walks on, and its
spawns, weapons, vehicles and flags become objects the simulation places. Then
it runs under the game's own multiplayer rules engine, the Megalo interpreter
Campaign Evolved still carries from Halo: Reach. Slayer and CTF are Megalo game
variants we write ourselves.

The one file that lists the maps for the game is built on your machine, from
your own installed copy, every time a map is installed and again after a game
update. Map packs are signed, and the launcher checks every signature and hash
before anything lands in your game folder.

## Known issues

- **Four players.** Fireteams top out at four for now. Raising that is the next
  thing we're working on. *Update: fixed in mods 0.10.0. Fireteams now take up
  to 16 players, each on their own PC.*
- **The end of a game** takes everyone back to the main menu. A post-game
  scoreboard and a vote for the next map are planned.
- **Finding games.** There's no public game browser in the game yet. Invite
  friends through your fireteam. Live games will also show on
  [mjolnircore.com/games](/games) as hosts start reporting them.
- **Game Pass.** Built and tested on the Steam version. The Xbox app version
  should work the same way but hasn't been tested yet.

Found something broken? Tell us on Discord, with your `ue4ss\UE4SS.log` if you
can. Every map is on the [Maps](/maps) page, each with its own page for
comments and ratings.
